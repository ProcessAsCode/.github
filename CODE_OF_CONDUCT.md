# Code of Conduct

The Process-as-Code organisation adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct for all repositories, issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass or demean anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@algomation.io**. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, including removing content or blocking participants.
